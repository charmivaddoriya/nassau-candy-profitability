# Executive Summary – Product Line Profitability

**Nassau Candy Distributor · Orders Jan 2024 – Dec 2025 · 10,194 order lines, 15 products, 3 divisions**

## Headline
The business earned **$93.4K gross profit on $141.8K sales (65.9% margin)**. Profit is healthy but **highly concentrated**: five Wonka Bar products deliver **95% of profit**.

## Key findings
1. **Chocolate carries the company.** It makes 92.9% of revenue and 95.1% of profit at a 67.5% margin.
2. **Sales volume and profit agree for the top five.** Each chocolate bar earns 17.8–20.7% of profit, with margins of 64.9–71.4%. Nutty Crunch Surprise has the best margin (71.4%); Milk Chocolate the lowest (64.9%).
3. **One product is a clear margin risk: Kazookles.** It sells 371 units at $3.25 against a $3.00 unit cost: a **7.7% margin** and only $0.25 profit per unit.
4. **The "Other" division under-earns.** It makes 6.8% of revenue but 4.6% of profit (44.8% margin), mainly because of Kazookles and Lickable Wallpaper (50% margin).
5. **Sugar is negligible.** Seven products together make 0.3% of revenue (only 137 units).
6. **Margins are stable.** Unit price and cost are fixed per product, so margin did not vary month to month, and the annual margin was 65.8% (2024) and 66.0% (2025). Growth (+45% sales in 2025) came from volume, not pricing.
7. **Supply dependency.** Lot's O' Nuts makes 56.5% of profit and Wicked Choccy's 38.6%.

## Recommendations
| Priority | Action | Why |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Reprice or renegotiate cost on **Kazookles**; if neither reaches ≥40% margin, review for discontinuation | Cost is 92% of price |
| 2 | Test a small price rise on **Lickable Wallpaper** | $7.9K sales at 50% margin; each +10% price adds about $0.8K profit |
| 3 | Review pricing on **Triple Dazzle Caramel** and **Milk Chocolate** (the two lowest-margin core bars; both sit below the 65.9% average) | They are 39% of profit, so small gains matter |
| 4 | **Consolidate the Sugar line** (7 SKUs, 0.3% of sales) or grow it deliberately | Adds complexity with almost no profit |
| 5 | Build **backup capacity** for the two chocolate factories | 95% of profit depends on them |

## Caveats
Gross margin only (no overheads or freight). Unit prices are fixed in the data, so the analysis cannot measure price elasticity. Sugar and some "Other" products have too few orders for firm conclusions.
